OffSignal Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 August 2026

OffSignal does not collect your data. What you send never leaves the two phones involved in a transfer. There are no accounts, no servers, and no analytics on transferred content.

What OffSignal does with what you send

OffSignal moves a note, link, or small file from one device to another by displaying a sequence of QR codes on one screen and reading them with the other device's camera. That transfer happens entirely over light.

  • Your message text, file contents, and file names exist only in memory for the duration of a session.
  • Nothing you send is uploaded, logged, or stored on any server.
  • Nothing is written to disk unless you explicitly choose Save or Share, which hands the file to your operating system at your request.
  • Closing the app discards the payload.

Camera

Receiving requires camera access so the app can read the QR codes on the other screen. Camera frames are processed on your device to decode the signal and are never recorded, saved, or transmitted. OffSignal only uses the camera while you are on the scanning screen, and releases it as soon as you stop, finish, leave the screen, or send the app to the background.

Settings stored on your device

OffSignal saves a small number of preferences locally on your device: your theme choice, your default cycle speed, whether haptics are on, and whether you have seen the intro. These never leave your device and contain nothing about what you have sent or received.

Network access

The Android app makes no network calls of any kind. It does not request the internet permission at all, so it cannot send anything anywhere even if it wanted to. You can verify that yourself in Android's app info screen.

There is no analytics, no crash reporting service, and no third-party SDK collecting anything. All fonts, icons, and animations are bundled with the app and are never fetched at runtime, so OffSignal works fully offline including in airplane mode.

Errors are logged locally on your device only, and record just the screen name and the type of error — never message text, file contents, or file names. Those logs never leave your device.

The web version is served from a static host over HTTPS. Loading the page is a normal web request; once loaded, the app itself makes no further network calls.

Children

OffSignal collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
